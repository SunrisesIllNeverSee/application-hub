# Applications in AQUA

Every application follows the same path, however it reaches AQUA:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| A. Choose | Pick a program from the catalog, import a web page, paste text, upload a file, or open one you started earlier. |
| B. Review | Check what AQUA found and fix anything it got wrong. |
| C. Create | AQUA makes your private copy of the application on this device. |
| D. Populate | AQUA compares each question with answers you already have. |
| E. Review answers | You approve each final answer yourself. |
| F. Prepare | AQUA checks that everything required is answered, approved and within limits. |
| G. Freeze and export | You lock the answers into a packet and save it as a file. |
| H. Record submission | You submit through the program's own site, then note that you did. |

Two things hold at every step:

- **Your answers stay on this device.** They live in your encrypted local
  vault. AQUA's servers never receive them.
- **AQUA never submits for you.** You submit through the program's own
  portal or email. AQUA only keeps your record of it.

Open **Start application** in the sidebar, or click **Start Application** on
any program page.

---

## 1. If you're new

1. Open **Start application**.
2. Pick one of the five ways to start (see the walkthroughs below).
3. When you reach **Create your private application**, AQUA asks you to
   create a vault secret. This is the password that encrypts your answers.
   It is never stored and never sent anywhere. If you lose it, your answers
   cannot be recovered, so keep it somewhere safe.
4. After that, unlock the vault with the same secret whenever you come back.

Not ready to write yet? Go as far as Step C and stop. Your private
application stays under **My applications**.

## 2. An application already in the catalog

1. Under **From the catalog**, search for the program and click **Start**.
2. Step B shows the template summary: program, cycle, deadline with time
   zone, and every question with its limits and whether it is required.
   Nothing here needs fixing. These are AQUA's published questions.
3. Click **Create private application**. Your copy only points at the
   catalog template. It does not duplicate it. Each program has one private
   application: if you already started one, from here or with
   **Start answer review** in the workspace, AQUA opens that one instead of
   making a duplicate.

## 3. A new application from a public web page

1. Under **From a URL**, paste the address of the public application page
   and click **Import**. If someone sent you an AQUA link that already
   carries the address, it opens with the address filled in. Nothing is
   fetched until you check it and click **Import**.
   - AQUA refuses links that look private, such as links with tokens,
     Google Docs edit links or share links. These are never sent. Download
     the form and use file import instead.
   - If the import fails, nothing is created. Try again or paste the
     questions instead.
2. Step B shows what AQUA found:
   - the program and, when AQUA recognises it, the matching catalog program
     with how sure it is;
   - the deadline with its time zone, and a warning if either is missing or
     ambiguous;
   - where the content came from and whether the page allows reuse;
   - each question in its exact wording, with limit and required chips and
     any matching known question.
3. Fix anything that's wrong. You can:
   - **Correct wording.** The original wording is kept and shown, and the
     correction is recorded.
   - Change **Required**, **Words** and **Chars**.
   - **Merge** a duplicate into the question it repeats. The stricter limit
     is kept.
   - **Remove** a question, or **Restore** it.
   - **Clear match**, or link or switch the catalog program.
4. If AQUA matched a catalog program, choose which list you will answer:
   - **The imported list:** it stays a separate private application, and
     the link to the program is kept with it.
   - **AQUA's catalog template:** this opens your one private application
     for that program.
5. Click **Create private application**.

**Optional: suggest it for the catalog.** For public pages that allow reuse,
you can also help other applicants:

1. Click **Show exactly what would be sent**. You'll see the full request:
   the program, the published deadline, the question wording and the source
   link.
2. To send it, tick the confirmation box and click **Send suggestion**.

The suggestion never includes your answers, approvals or corrections, or
anything marked private or uncertain. AQUA fetches the public page again
itself to check it. A catalog curator reviews every suggestion before
anything is published. If the page turns out to forbid reuse, or AQUA cannot
reach it, you'll see a message and nothing is added. If you see "Catalog submissions
open soon", nothing was sent.

## 4. A private application from your own files

Use **Paste text** or **Upload a file** for forms you received privately, a
document you drafted, or a page you can't share.

- **Paste text:** paste the questions and click **Find the questions**.
- **Upload a file:** choose a .txt, .md, .html, .csv, .json or .docx file
  (up to 5 MB). PDFs aren't supported yet. Copy the questions out and paste
  them, or save the form as .docx.

The text is read on your device and is never uploaded. AQUA matches the
questions against the catalog it already has on the page. A private import
never becomes public catalog material, and it cannot be suggested for the
catalog. Review and correct it as in section 3, then create it. AQUA gives it
a private id starting with `LA-`.

## 5. Reusing answers you already have

On your application page, **Step D** lists every question with a label:

- **Direct**: you have an approved answer to the same question that fits
  the limits. **Use this answer** copies it in as a new draft.
  **Edit before using** lets you change it first.
- **Adapt**: you have a related answer that needs changes. AQUA says why,
  for example "over the word limit", "mentions another program" or "refers
  to an old year". **Adapt this answer** opens it with that advice.
- **Gap**: you have nothing related yet. **Write a draft** opens an empty
  editor.

**Draft with my AI key (optional)** uses your own AI provider key from the
vault. You see exactly what will be sent before anything goes out.

Reusing an answer never carries its approval over. Every reused answer is a
new draft that you approve again for this application. Changing an answer
adds a new version, and earlier versions and their decisions stay as they
were. **Record answer-bank matches** saves AQUA's suggestions with the
application. They are advice only and approve nothing.

## 6. Reviewing your final answers

**Step E** shows each question's status: gap, suggested, under review,
approved, and so on. Click **Review answers** to open the review board,
where you approve each answer exactly as shown. On the board you can also
export a review file to read offline and import your decisions back.

- Only you can approve an answer.
- An approval applies only to the exact version you approved.
- If the answer changes afterwards, it needs a new approval.
- Suggestions and AI drafts can never approve anything.

## 7. Exporting and submitting

1. **Step F, Prepare**, lists anything still blocking you, with a link to
   fix each one:
   - a required question with no answer, or no approved answer;
   - an answer over its word or character limit;
   - an approved answer whose stored text no longer matches what you
     approved;
   - an optional question left out without a reason. Type the reason here.
2. When it says **ready to freeze**, go to **Step G**:
   - **Freeze packet** locks the selected approved answers into a packet
     with a fingerprint (manifest hash and packet id).
   - Optionally tick the sign-off box and **Sign packet**. The signature is
     made on your device.
   - **Export packet (local file)** saves the packet as a file on your
     device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
3. Anyone can check an exported packet offline:
   `node app/lib/aqua-local/verify-packet.mjs your-packet.json`
   It rechecks every hash and the signature. Exit code 0 means everything
   matches.
4. Submit through the program's own portal or email. AQUA cannot do this
   for you.
5. Back in the packet panel, fill in **Record external submission**: how
   you submitted, when, and the confirmation number if you have one. Then
   click **Record submission**. This is your own statement that you
   submitted. AQUA does not claim it submitted anything.

## 8. Reopening past work

Open **Start application**, then **My applications**. Each application shows
its status:

- **active**: still being prepared.
- **queued**: a packet is frozen or exported, but no submission is recorded
  yet.
- **submitted**: you recorded a submission.

Use the filters to show one status at a time. A submitted application opens
read-only. It shows the frozen packet with its fingerprint and signature
status, the exact answers you sent, and your submission record. Everything
stays in your vault on this device. Keep a vault backup (Local vault → back
up) if you want it on another device.
