# Question Profile: Tell me about yourself

## Basics

- **Question text:** Tell me about yourself
- **Universal theme:** background
- **Domain:** general (appears in all domains)
- **Significance score:** 0.98 (the most universal question across all application types)
- **Is universal:** true
- **Typical word limit:** varies (50-500 depending on context)
- **Asked by:** Nearly every program, job interview, school application

## Why it matters

This is the most universal question in the archive. It appears in every domain — founder applications, job interviews, school essays, grant proposals. The answer sets the frame for everything that follows. A weak opening makes every subsequent answer harder.

## What a good answer contains

- Present: who you are and what you're building/doing now
- Past: the relevant experience that led here (not your full history)
- Future: where you're going and why this opportunity matters
- A hook: one specific, memorable detail that makes you different from every other applicant

## Common failure modes

- Starting with childhood or early education (too far back)
- Reciting a resume chronologically (no narrative)
- No connection to the specific opportunity (generic)
- Too long (the answer should end before the listener wants it to)
