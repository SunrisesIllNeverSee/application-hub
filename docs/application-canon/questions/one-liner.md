# Question Profile: What does your startup do in one sentence?

## Basics

- **Question text:** What does your startup do in one sentence?
- **Universal theme:** approach
- **Domain:** founder (also appears in jobs as "elevator pitch")
- **Significance score:** 0.96 (critical — this is the first thing every program reads)
- **Is universal:** true (appears across most programs)
- **Typical word limit:** 25 words
- **Asked by:** Y Combinator, 500 Global, Techstars, Alchemist Accelerator

## Why it matters

This is the single most-asked question in the archive. It's the first impression. If the one-liner is unclear, the reader stops. It needs to communicate: what you build, who it's for, and what's novel — in under 25 words.

## What a good answer contains

- The product category (not the technology)
- The target user
- The core value or differentiation
- No jargon, no buzzwords, no hedging

## Common failure modes

- Describing the technology instead of the product
- Using category names that require explanation
- Trying to fit multiple products into one sentence
- Vague verbs ("we help", "we enable")
