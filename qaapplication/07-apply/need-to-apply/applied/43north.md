## Application

**What specific problem are you solving?**
We build execution-grade AI governance infrastructure for the headless, agentic CLI future. AI has no cross-session memory, no accountability, no self-constraint against drift. Every fix is pre-execution (policies) or post-execution (audits); none govern at the moment of action. MO§ES™ does: it works inside the token-generation loop and realigns to intent before a rogue action forms, on the Conservation Law of Commitment, a falsifiable law for how meaning survives, unbroken for four months.

**Who are your ideal customers?**
Two surfaces from one substrate. Beachhead: AI power users, developers, and creators who do sustained AI work and want to measure and rank their performance. That is SigRank, low-CAC and share-worthy. Expansion: enterprise AI teams running multi-agent systems who need auditable, governed execution. Their pain: models forget across sessions, agents drift from roles, nothing is enforced at execution, no standard measures governance quality. MO§ES™ solves all four, engine-agnostic.

**Please link to a product demo video.**
mos2es.com/demovideo

**Why do you think now is the right time for your product or solution?**
Three forces converge in 2026. Agent commerce moves into production, so agents transact, not just chat, and ungoverned execution becomes a real liability. Regulation arrives: the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF mandate auditability, yet no benchmark for governance quality exists. And the cost curve flipped: a solo operator now ships production code at $0.0007 per line, so the bottleneck is trusting what was built, not building it. Whoever names and instruments the category first owns it.

**What is your customer acquisition strategy?**
Founder-led first. The Conservation Law preprint is citable and drives organic discovery among researchers and developers. The benchmarks are shareable and verifiable: "97.23% cache hit, here is the receipt" is the kind of number developers repost. My X (x.com/burnmydays) and warm inbound from The Pitch by Deel feed the funnel. SigRank is the engine: ranks and performance cards spread in technical communities. Enterprise is direct outreach once SigRank shows traction.

**What is your TAM, SAM, and SOM? Please provide your calculations for each of these**
TAM: ~$20B to $25B today, $90B to $115B+ as AI compounds, across AI governance tooling ($10B+ by 2028, Gartner), AI-native dev infra (Copilot alone $1B+ ARR; segment >$50B in 5yr), and AI evaluation (unowned). SAM: SigRank ($3B to $7B) plus MO§ES™ licensing ($0.3B to $0.9B), ~$4B to $8B. SOM: 50,000 SigRank operators x $20 x 12 = ~$12M ARR, plus 20 enterprise licenses x $50K = $1M, so $10M to $25M ARR in three years.

**Who are your current competitors and how do you differentiate yourself?**
Orchestration is not governance. All sit outside the model, patching after the fact: inline proxies (Exogram, Cerone, Prisma AIRS) intercept after logic collapses; frozen-brain cages (LLM-NANO-VM, Lean-Agent) suffocate reasoning into flowcharts; middleware hooks (Zenity, Agent Control Standard) see the tool call, not the drift; sandboxes (Cloudflare) and post-hoc audits (Obsidian) fence or autopsy. MO§ES™ governs inside the token loop, before the action fires. Patent pending 19/426,028.
