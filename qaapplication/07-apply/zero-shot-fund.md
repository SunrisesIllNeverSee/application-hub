## What are you building?

SigRank is a live, privacy-preserving operator-performance index for AI users.
It measures operator yield and human-AI synchronization without exposing private
prompt content. Most AI evaluation measures models, prompts, or outputs; SigRank
adds the missing operator layer.

## Why now / why this matters

AI is becoming routine in knowledge work and agent-mediated workflows, but teams
cannot yet measure whether people are using it effectively without turning private
working context into a surveillance product. SigRank makes that performance
visible, improvable, and eventually auditable.

## Evidence and raise

SigRank is live at https://signalaf.com with 1,628 seeded operators and 40+
active users after its marketing launch. We are raising a $375K pre-seed SAFE to
prove repeat use, convert a first paid design partner, and launch an initial
fintech/compliance pilot.
