## What we are building

SigRank is a live, privacy-preserving operator-performance index for AI users.
It measures operator yield and human-AI synchronization without exposing private
prompt content—the missing human layer in AI evaluation.

## Traction and raise

SigRank has 1,628 seeded operators and 40+ active users. We are raising a $375K
pre-seed SAFE to prove repeat use, convert a first paid design partner, and run
an initial fintech/compliance pilot.

## Why Village

We are early and cross-sector by design: the live wedge is operator measurement,
with a longer-term governed-execution infrastructure opportunity. Village’s
global, pre-traction/pre-revenue posture and operator network are a strong fit.
