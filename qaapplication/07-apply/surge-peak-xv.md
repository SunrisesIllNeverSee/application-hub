## One-sentence description

SigRank measures human-AI operator performance without exposing private prompt
content.

## Problem

AI teams can benchmark models and inspect outputs, but lack a privacy-preserving
way to measure whether people are using AI effectively and repeatably.

## What is unique

SigRank adds the missing operator layer to AI evaluation: it measures operator
yield and human-AI synchronization while keeping private prompt content private.

## Founder / traction / raise

Deric McHenry is a solo founder with a video-game-and-statistics background that
led him to make AI performance visible and improvable. SigRank is live with
1,628 seeded operators and 40+ active users. No outside capital or revenue has
been raised; the active raise is a $375K pre-seed SAFE.
