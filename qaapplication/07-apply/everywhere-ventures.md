## Concise company overview

SigRank is a live, privacy-preserving operator-performance index for AI users.
It measures how effectively people work with AI without exposing private prompt
content. The product has 1,628 seeded operators and 40+ active users.

## Opportunity and raise

AI work is becoming economically consequential, but teams cannot measure the
human operator without creating a surveillance product. We are raising a $375K
pre-seed SAFE to prove repeat use, secure a first paid design partner, and begin
an initial fintech/compliance pilot.

## Why Everywhere

The company is designed to begin outside traditional AI-infrastructure hubs and
benefit from a global community of founders and operators.
