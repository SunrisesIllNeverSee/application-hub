## Company Overview

SigRank is a live operator-performance index for AI users. It measures operator yield and human-AI synchronization without exposing private prompt content. Most AI evaluation measures models, prompts, or outputs; SigRank adds the missing operator layer, helping individuals and teams understand how effectively people direct AI systems. MO§ES™ is the underlying measurement and governance infrastructure, which can later verify whether meaning and commitments survive as agents transform language and take action.

## Founder's Personal Story

My background in video games and statistics made the missing operator layer obvious to me: games make performance visible and improvable, while AI products mostly measure the model. I built SigRank to test that idea in a live product. The work led to published Commitment Theory research, public test harnesses, patent filings, and the broader MO§ES™ infrastructure. I am a solo founder and open to the right co-founder.

## Motivation

AI is becoming part of everyday work, but people and teams cannot yet measure whether human-AI collaboration is effective without exposing private working context. I want to make that performance visible, improvable, and eventually auditable as AI systems move from assistance into agent-mediated action.

## Future insight

AI performance will be measured as a relationship between an operator and a system, not only as a property of the model. The winning products will improve that relationship while protecting the context that makes human work private.

## Problem

AI teams can benchmark models and inspect system traces, but they lack a privacy-preserving standard for measuring the human operator: who is using AI effectively, what practices create better outcomes, and whether that performance is repeatable. Token volume, thumbs-up signals, and model benchmarks do not answer those questions.
