# Master Investor Answers — SigRank-led

## Narrative hierarchy

1. **Live wedge:** SigRank measures operator yield and human-AI
   synchronization without requiring access to private prompt content.
2. **Evidence:** the product is live, with 1,628 seeded operators and 40+
   active users after its marketing launch.
3. **Commercial test:** prove repeated use and a paid design-partner use case
   with a defined operator group, employer, or team.
4. **Infrastructure expansion:** MO§ES™ applies the same measurement substrate
   to governed execution and preserving meaning across agent workflows.

## Company one-liner

SigRank is an operator-performance index for AI users: it measures how people
work with AI without exposing their private prompt content.

## What are you building?

We are building SigRank, a live product for measuring operator yield and
human-AI synchronization. Today, most AI evaluation measures models, prompts,
or outputs; it does not measure how effectively a person directs an AI system.
SigRank creates that missing operator layer while protecting private prompt
content. MO§ES™ is the underlying measurement and governance infrastructure:
over time, the same substrate can verify whether meaning and commitments
survive as agents transform language and take action.

## Current traction

SigRank is live at `signalaf.com`. As of 2026-08-03, it has 1,628 seeded
operators and 40+ active users since the marketing launch two weeks earlier.
Usage is free; there are no paying users and revenue is $0. The broader work
also includes published Commitment Theory research, public test harnesses,
patent filings, and live product surfaces. The next proof point is repeat use
by a defined operator group and a clear paid use case.

## Problem

AI teams can benchmark models and inspect system traces, but they lack a
privacy-preserving standard for measuring the human operator: who is using AI
effectively, what practices create better outcomes, and whether that performance
is repeatable. Token volume, thumbs-up signals, and model benchmarks do not
answer those questions. This leaves individuals, employers, and AI-native teams
without a useful way to measure operator performance or improve it.

## Why now?

AI is moving from occasional experimentation into everyday knowledge work and
agent-mediated workflows. As usage becomes economically important, measuring
the model alone is insufficient: organizations need a way to understand and
improve the human-AI system without turning private working context into a
surveillance product.

## Why this founder?

My background in video games and statistics led me to see the missing operator
layer: games make performance visible and improvable, while AI products mostly
measure the model. I built SigRank to test that idea in a live product. Building
the measurement system led to Commitment Theory and the broader MO§ES™
infrastructure, which focuses on whether meaningful instructions survive as
language is transformed and agents act.

performance or preserve evidence across agent workflows. Do not claim a buyer,
