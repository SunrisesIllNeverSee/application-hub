## Questions

1. What founding role do you see yourself in?
2. Where are you in your founder journey?
3. Which sectors would you like to found a company in? (Choose up to 3)
4. What have you done that’s genuinely impressive?
5. What’s the riskiest decision you’ve made? Why did you make it?
6. What’s the first thing you ever built or sold?
7. What's something weird or niche you were obsessed with? How deep did you go?
8. Who are 1–2 people who can vouch for you? (Name, email, relationship)
9. Great founders tend to know other great founders. Anyone early in their journey who should be on our radar?
10. Are you prepared to commit full-time to Catalyst in San Francisco for 8 weeks, with no side projects or consulting?
