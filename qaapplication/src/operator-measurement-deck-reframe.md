# Operator Measurement Deck Reframe

## Intent

Preserve the MO§ES™ constitutional architecture while making SigRank, now live at Signalaf, the empirical front door.

## Changes applied

1. **Opening:** contrasts performative operator benchmarks with live measurement and makes operator performance the headline.
2. **Problem:** replaces the generic execution bottleneck with the missing privacy-preserving operator-performance standard.
3. **Stack:** keeps MO§ES™, AQUA, SigRank, and SIGNOMY/CIVITAE as one system, but identifies SigRank as the active measurement wedge.
4. **Proof:** moves from aggregate token volume to the measurement instrument, operator rankings, and the privacy model.
5. **Market:** prioritizes the initial buyer: teams and employers that need to identify high-yield AI operation without exposing prompts.
6. **Sequence:** turns the seven revenue lines into a causal progression: live measurement → paid team calibration → enterprise governed-execution pilot → constitutional infrastructure.
7. **Ask:** replaces the $1.5M SAFE with a $375K first SAFE to establish proof of life: repeat operator use, a paid design partner, and the first fintech/compliance pilot.

## Non-negotiable thesis

SigRank is live operational measurement, not a subjective retrospective audit. MO§ES™ is the science and governed-execution layer that makes this measurement durable, privacy-preserving, and expandable into agent-economy infrastructure.
