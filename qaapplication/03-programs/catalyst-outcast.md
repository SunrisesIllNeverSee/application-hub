# Catalyst from Outcast Ventures

- **url:** https://catalyst.outcastventures.com
- **operator:** Outcast Ventures
- **type:** founder-formation program
- **format:** 8 weeks, in person, San Francisco
- **dates:** 2026-09-16 through 2026-11-11
- **application deadline:** 2026-08-02, 11:59pm PT
- **stage:** individuals seeking an intentional co-founder; not formed teams
- **status:** drafting

## Description

Catalyst brings together technical and non-technical individuals who have built something substantial and are serious about choosing a co-founder. Participants work from the same San Francisco office for eight weeks. Outcast may invest if a team forms through the program.

## Fit note

Deric is applying as a CEO/product-and-operations founder seeking a technical co-founder. The full-time, no-side-project commitment requires an explicit decision before submission because MO§ES™ is an active solo company.
